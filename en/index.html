Worked example

Weld calculation for a tube under torsion

A typical case in machine and steel construction: a tube welded all round to a plate is twisted. This is how DT-ProfiSchweissnaht checks the circular weld.

Example: round tube under torsion – circular fillet weld to EN 1993-1-8

Round tube Ø 114.3 × 6 mm welded to a plate, fillet weld all round with throat a = 4 mm, S235, torque T = 9 kNm. Under torsion the shear stress runs along the weld (τ∥); the outer edge of the weld governs.

Wt = Ip / rmax = 79,408 mm³
τ∥ = T / Wt = 9,000,000 / 79,408 = 113.3 N/mm²
σv = √3 · τ∥ = 196.3 N/mm² ≤ 360 N/mm²
η = 196.3 / 360 = 0.55 ✓

Recalculate the example yourself

In the free version, choose “Circular tube 114.3×6 on plate, circular weld, torsion 9 kNm” under “Load example” and press “Calculate”. The calculation path shows all 36 steps with checks. With “Design (throat sought)” the program determines the required throat thickness instead. A classic mechanical-engineering variant (Roloff/Matek) with 6 kNm is included as well.

FAQ

Questions about DT-ProfiSchweissnaht

Which standard does DT-ProfiSchweissnaht use?

EN 1993-1-8 (Eurocode 3) and the classic methods of mechanical engineering; heat control to EN 1011-2.

Which welds are supported?

Fillet welds and butt welds with the matching weld preparations; the weld layout is drawn to scale.

Can I calculate a tube weld under torsion?

Yes. For round tubes the program checks the circular weld all round using the polar moment of area and searches for the maximum stress at 72 points along the weld. A ready-made example (round tube 114.3 × 6 mm, torque 9 kNm) can be loaded directly.

What does the program not check?

The program lists explicitly what it does not check – for example verifications outside the weld itself.

What does the free version do?

It calculates everything. Only outputs are locked: save, open, print and the Word report. It may be passed on freely.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The free version runs directly in the browser, without sign-up or installation.

Does the program work offline?

Yes. All calculations run on your own device; no data is sent to any server.
